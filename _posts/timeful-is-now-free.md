Starting today, Timeful is completely free to use. We got rid of the banner ads, and we got rid of Timeful Premium.

That means:

- **No more ads.** The banner and video ads are gone from every page.
- **Unlimited events.** There's no longer a cap on how many events you can create.
- **Every feature, for everyone.** Everything that used to be behind the paywall is now available to all users.

There's nothing to sign up for and nothing to change. If you're already using Timeful, it just got better.

## Why we did this

When we started building Timeful (back when it was still called [Schej](/blog/schej-is-now-timeful)), we just wanted a better When2meet: something clean, fast, and easy to use. Over time, the ads and the upgrade prompts started getting in the way of exactly that.

Finding a time to meet should be simple. You shouldn't have to look at annoying ads or hit a paywall to do it.

## What about existing Premium subscribers?

If you were paying for Timeful Premium: thank you. Your support helped keep Timeful running. Your subscription has been cancelled, and you won't be charged again.

Previous subscribers also gain access to a special limited edition "Supporter" badge, as well as a glowing ring around their profile picture in all events they are a part of.

## We're still open source

Timeful is [open source on GitHub](https://github.com/schej-it/timeful.app). If you want to support the project, the best way is to give us a star or share Timeful with your friends, classmates, and coworkers.

Happy scheduling!
