If you're searching for a simple way to find a meeting time that works for everyone, chances are you've run into **When2meet** and **Doodle**. These two tools are among the most popular for group scheduling—but which one is actually better?

In this post, we'll compare **When2meet vs Doodle**, break down their pros and cons, and introduce a third option—**Timeful (formerly Schej)**—that might be an even better fit in 2025.

## When2meet: Quick and Lightweight

![When2meet scheduling interface](/blog/assets/blog/when2meet-vs-doodle/when2meet.png)

[When2meet](https://when2meet.com) is beloved for its speed and simplicity. You don't need an account, you don't need to sign in, and the interface—while outdated—is incredibly functional. Just send out a link, have everyone click when they're available, and boom—you're done.

**Pros**:

- No signups required
- Very fast setup
- Clean availability grid

**Cons**:

- UI is visually dated
- No calendar integration
- No email notifications or reminders

**Best for**: Study groups, casual meetups, and quick polls where you don't need bells and whistles.

---

## Doodle: Familiar but Frustrating

![Doodle scheduling interface](/blog/assets/blog/when2meet-vs-doodle/doodle.png)

[Doodle](https://doodle.com) is one of the oldest scheduling tools on the internet. It's polished and comes with integrations like Outlook and Google Calendar, but over time it's become bloated and commercialized.

Many users complain about:

- Misleading ads that look like buttons
- Required signups for some features
- A clunky process just to create a basic poll
- Confusing final tallies when comparing times

If you need calendar sync or detailed availability features, Doodle can still work—but it's no longer the fast, friendly tool it once was.

**Best for**: Enterprise teams already paying for it, or people who need calendar integrations.

---

## Timeful (formerly Schej): A Better Alternative in 2025

![Timeful scheduling interface](/blog/assets/blog/when2meet-vs-doodle/timeful.png)

[Timeful](https://timeful.app) was built as a response to all the frustrations people have with Doodle. It's fast, open source, privacy-friendly, and doesn't shove ads or upsells in your face.

Like When2meet, there's no need to sign up, but unlike When2meet, it's modern, responsive, and easier to read.

**Why people are switching to Timeful**:

- No misleading ads
- Clean, modern UI that works on desktop and mobile
- Minimal clicks required to create a poll and share the link
- Everyone's availability is clearly visible at a glance
- Calendar integration and email notifications + reminders
- Open source

Timeful is ideal for small teams, remote groups, students, and anyone who's tired of slow or cluttered scheduling tools.

Try it here: [https://timeful.app](https://timeful.app)

## Verdict: When2meet vs Doodle vs Timeful

- Use **When2meet** if you need something super fast and don't mind the barebones look.
- Use **Doodle** if you need calendar integration and don't mind a few ads or extra steps.
- Use **Timeful** if you want the best of both worlds—speed, clarity, and a clean, ad-free experience.

---

## Final Thoughts

Choosing a scheduling tool shouldn't feel like a chore. Whether you've been frustrated by Doodle's complexity or find When2meet's UI lacking, **Timeful** offers a refreshing alternative.

If you're organizing meetings regularly and want something that just works—give it a try.
